Drop-In vs Appt. Checklist

Drop-In Space:

Triaging multiple students, 10-15 min limit per check-in, active roaming, focus on setting up a task then walking away.

Appointment Space:

50-minute dedicated focus, deep diagnostics, agenda setting, active check-ins, action plans for post-session study.

Welcome To the Learning Commons [cite: 1, 2]

The Foundation of Peer Tutoring

Peer tutoring is not about acting as an all-knowing professor, nor is it about doing a student's homework for them. According to the College Reading and Learning Association (CRLA) standard, your primary goal is to **facilitate independent learning**.

Our Primary Mission

Teach the student *how* to think, study, and solve, rather than just providing answers.

Two Service Landscapes

Adapt smoothly between structured 50-minute appointments and active drop-in hubs.
